Why doesn't this page just scan my installed apps?

Browsers don't let a website read the list of apps on your phone — that's a privacy boundary, not a bug. The closest real equivalent is "Pay with any UPI app" above: it fires the standard UPI intent, and your phone's own operating system shows every UPI app you actually have installed. That list comes from your phone, not from this page.

How is "verified" decided here?

As a browser tab, this page can tell when you switch away to another app and when you come back — nothing more. It cannot see your bank, your UPI app, or whether money actually moved. So after you return, it asks you directly whether the payment went through, and shows that as the result.

A production-grade version would need a backend connected to a licensed UPI payment aggregator (for example Razorpay, Cashfree, or PhonePe's gateway), which confirms each transaction by ID via a server-to-server webhook — the only trustworthy source of truth for "did this payment succeed."
